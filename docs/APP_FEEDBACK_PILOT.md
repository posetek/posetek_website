## Task script

Use [the public preview](https://posetek.net/feedback?preview=1). Confirm the
**Preview · nothing you enter here will be sent** label. Preview tasks send no
feedback to production. Keep observation notes anonymous; do not copy identifying
text, record screens containing account details or collect the child's actual
free-text response. A child can decline or stop at any point.

1. **First use:** Say, “This is a preview, so nothing you enter will be sent.
   Please try it as you normally would. You can stop whenever you want.” Let the
   child read and answer according to their experience, without explaining the
   questions or suggesting answers. Record assistance separately. Time from the
   first question appearing to confirmation, the all-skipped ending or their exit.
   Include reading time; record whole seconds. Do not include follow-up tasks.
2. **Not used:** On a fresh preview, ask them to try **Haven’t used it yet** and
   send that answer. Check whether they understand why no app ratings are asked.
   This is a route check, not a claim that they have never used PoseTek. If their
   first task already used this path, do not repeat it.
3. **Back, skips and exit:** On a fresh preview, ask them to choose an answer,
   advance and return to change it. Then ask them to skip all three questions and
   leave the comment blank. Check that they recognize **No feedback sent**.
   Ask how they would leave without sending; let them use **Back to PoseTek**.
4. **Keyboard, where applicable:** On their usual keyboard device, try Tab,
   arrow-key radio selection, Enter, Back and Skip. Record unaided/help/blocked,
   or N/A if their device has no keyboard. A touch-only participant does not fail
   a keyboard task.

After the timed task, ask without supplying the expected answer:

- “What was each question asking you to tell us?” Record Q1/Q2/Q3 separately as
  understood unaided, needed explanation, or unclear; note the confusing word.
- “What would you do if none of the choices fitted?”
- “What happens if you skip or leave? Would that affect your results or workouts?”
- “Who reads this, and what kind of information would you leave out?”
- “When would you want to be asked? How often would feel okay?” Then explain the
  current once-every-seven-days automatic invitation after an acknowledged
  completed workout save, and ask whether that sounds appropriate. Record their
  view, not a demonstrated seven-day outcome from this single session.

If the normal session happens to show a real workout invitation, observe whether
**Not now** dismisses it and results remain available. Record only a deidentified
note; do not create extra workouts or send production feedback for this check.
